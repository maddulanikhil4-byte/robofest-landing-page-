ABOUT ROBOFEST

Big ideas.
Real-world impact.

ROBOFEST is a proposed robotics and technology festival concept for ROBOTiX Club, NIT Raipur. It brings students together to learn, build, experiment and compete through hands-on challenges.

Whether you are taking your first step into robotics or improving a project, the goal is simple: turn curiosity into creation.
